Accessibility
Accessability
LEGiON Platforms is committed to providing equal access to our products and services for all individuals, including those with disabilities. We believe that accessibility is a fundamental aspect of good design and user experience, and we are dedicated to making our products and services usable by everyone. The following policy serves as a guide to ensure that accessibility is integrated into all aspects of our operations. Compliance with Standards: LEGiON Platforms will comply with all relevant accessibility standards, including but not limited to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.0, and the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA). Accessible Design: LEGiON Platforms will incorporate accessibility into the design and development of all our products and services from the beginning, and will conduct regular accessibility testing throughout the development process to ensure that our products are usable by everyone. Employee Training: LEGiON Platforms will provide training to all employees on accessibility and assistive technologies, to ensure that they have the knowledge and skills necessary to create and maintain accessible products and services. Alternate Formats: LEGiON Platforms will provide alternative formats, such as audio descriptions, subtitles, and braille, to individuals with disabilities upon request. Accessibility Feedback: LEGiON Platforms will provide an accessible feedback mechanism, such as an email address or a phone number, for individuals with disabilities to report accessibility barriers and request assistance. Continuous Improvement: LEGiON Platforms will review and update our accessibility policy and practices on an ongoing basis, to ensure that our products and services are accessible to all individuals, including those with disabilities. By following this accessibility policy, LEGiON Platforms will ensure that our products and services are accessible to everyone, and uphold its reputation as a company that is committed to providing equal access and user experience for all individuals, including those with disabilities.
