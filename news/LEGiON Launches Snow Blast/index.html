LEGiON Launches Snow Blast
December 20, 2022
(LEGiON Platforms) - LEGiON Platforms is excited to announce the release of SnowBlast, a 3D multiplayer snowball fight game, just in time for the Christmas event. The game allows players to engage in thrilling snowball battles with friends and other players from around the world. SnowBlast features realistic winter environments and physics-based gameplay, making for a truly immersive gaming experience. In addition, the game will have different game modes and customizations options, allowing players to tailor their gameplay experience. SnowBlast is now available to play for free in web browsers and is sure to be a hit for the holidays.
